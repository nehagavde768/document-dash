# Documents Required for Certificates

**Chhattisgarh · प्रमाण पत्र हेतु आवश्यक दस्तावेज़**

Domicile, Income and the caste certificates follow the Chhattisgarh Rajpatra of 28 July 2025.
Marriage follows the marriage registration table.

> **How to read this:** each certificate needs every document type listed for it.
> For each type, **any one** of the accepted documents is enough.

## At a glance

| # | Certificate | Issued by | Document types needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Domicile Certificate**<br>निवास प्रमाण पत्र | Office of the Tehsildar | 4 |
| 2 | **Income Certificate**<br>आय प्रमाण पत्र | Office of the Tehsildar | 3 (+1 optional) |
| 3 | **OBC Caste Certificate**<br>अन्य पिछड़ा वर्ग जाति प्रमाण पत्र | Office of the Tehsildar | 6 |
| 4 | **SC / ST Caste Certificate**<br>अनुसूचित जाति / जनजाति प्रमाण पत्र | Office of the Tehsildar | 5 |
| 5 | **Marriage Certificate**<br>विवाह प्रमाण पत्र | Office of the Registrar of Marriages | 8 |

## Good to know

- **Shared documents:** the Affidavit (Notarized) is needed for Income, both caste certificates and Marriage. The Application Form is needed for Income and both caste certificates. The Patwari Report and Family Tree are needed for both caste certificates.
- **Caste cut-off dates:** the ancestors' records must date from before **26.12.1984** for OBC, **10.08.1950** for SC and **06.09.1950** for ST.
- **OBC only:** the OBC Caste Certificate also needs an Income Certificate.
- **Income:** salary proof (Form 16 / ITR / salary certificate) is needed only for government servants.
